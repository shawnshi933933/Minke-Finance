# How It Works

Minke converts illiquid future-income positions into tradable tokens. No oracles. No counterparty. No haircuts.

## The Flow

```
1. SETUP      — A project creates a Vesting Suite--a Vault and ERC1155NFT contract
2. MINT VT    — Recipient stakes NFT → Vault mints VT into their wallet
3. TRADE/HOLD — Holder sells VTs on AMM, stakes in BuybackPool, or holds to maturity
4. REDEEM     — VT holder redeems at maturity 1:1 via redeemVT()
```

Each step is a permissionless on-chain action. There is no centralized intermediary at any step.

## Step 1: Project Setup

A project creates a **Vesting Suite** via `VestingFactory` — this deploys a `Vault` and `ERC1155NFT` contract. The project owner then:
- Deposits the underlying tokens (T) into the Vault
- Creates tranches (each with its own `VTToken` and `BuybackPool`)
- Mints ERC-1155 NFTs representing each recipient's vesting allocation and distributes them

## Step 2: Mint VT

Recipients stake their ERC-1155 allocation NFTs into the Vault:

```solidity
function stakeNFT(uint256 trancheId, uint256 amount) external;
```

The Vault mints VT to the recipient at the tranche's fixed rate (`vtPerNft`). VTs are standard ERC-20 tokens — transferable, listable, composable.

## Step 3: Trade, Stake, or Hold

VT holders have three options:

| Action | Mechanism | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Sell** | List VT on any AMM at a discount | Immediate liquidity |
| **Stake** | Stake VT in the BuybackPool | Receive T gradually as it vests (e.g. 5% fee) |
| **Hold** | Keep VTs until maturity | Redeem at par 1:1 in Phase 2 |

### Staking in the BuybackPool

When a VT holder stakes in the BuybackPool, the pool automatically pulls T from the Vault per the release schedule, burns VT 1:1 as T arrives, and distributes T to stakers proportionally minus the protocol fee (e.g. 5%). The holder calls `claimT()` to receive their accumulated T.

VT buyers on secondary markets make a time-value-of-money bet: they acquire future claims at a discount and either stake for gradual T receipt or redeem at par at maturity.

## Step 4: Phase 2 Redemption

Once the vesting schedule completes, `vestingEnded` is set to `true` on the BuybackPool. Any VT holder can call:

```solidity
function redeemVT(uint256 vtAmount) external;
```

This burns `vtAmount` VT 1:1 from the BuybackPool's `excessT` reserve and transfers T to the caller. No protocol fee applies to Phase 2 redemption.

```
redeemed = 1 underlying per VT  (always exactly 1:1)
```

<Callout type="tip" title="No oracles, no liquidations">
At no point does Minke consult an external price feed. The 1:1 backing guarantee is mathematical — not economic — making the system immune to oracle manipulation and collateral cascades.
</Callout>
