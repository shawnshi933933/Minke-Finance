# How It Works

Minke converts illiquid future-income positions into tradable tokens in four steps. No oracles. No counterparty. No haircuts.

## The Four-Step Flow

```
1. DEPOSIT    — Holder deposits future-income position into Minke Protocol
2. RECEIVE VT — Protocol mints VTs (1:1 with underlying) into holder's wallet
3. TRADE/HOLD — Holder sells VTs at a discount, stakes them, or holds to maturity
4. REDEEM     — VT holder redeems at maturity for underlying 1:1
```

Each step is a permissionless on-chain action. There is no centralized intermediary in any step.

## Step 1: Deposit

A protocol team creates a new `VestingToken` for the tranche. Investor, or individual deposit a vesting position.


## Step 2: Receive VT

Immediately after deposit, the depositor receives Vesting Tokens at a **1:1 ratio** with the net deposited amount.


VTs are standard ERC-20 tokens. They can be:
- Transferred to any wallet
- Listed on Uniswap, Curve, or any AMM
- Deposited into lending protocols as collateral
- Sold OTC to buyers seeking discounted future exposure

## Step 3: Trade, Hold, or Stake

The depositor now chooses what to do with their VTs:

| Action | Mechanism | Outcome |
|---|---|---|
| **Sell** | List on AMM at a discount | Immediate liquidity, give up future upside |
| **Hold** | Keep VTs | Appreciate as maturity approaches |
| **Stake** | Call `stakeVT()` | Earn pro-rata unlocks during Phase 1 |

VT buyers on secondary markets are making a time-value-of-money bet: they acquire future claims at a discount and redeem at par at maturity.

## Step 4: Redeem

Once the vesting schedule ends, the protocol calls `endVesting()` which:
1. Locks Phase 1 staking
2. Calculates total excess tokens (unclaimed underlying)
3. Opens Phase 2 — `redeemVT()` is now available

In Phase 2, any VT holder calls `redeemVT(amount)` and receives:
```
redeemed = amount × (excessT / totalVT)
```

Because VTs are 1:1 backed (and excess may have accumulated from unclaimed rewards), holders typically receive **≥ 1 underlying per VT**.

<Callout type="tip" title="No oracles, no liquidations">
At no point does Minke consult an external price feed. The 1:1 backing guarantee is mathematical — not economic — making the system immune to oracle manipulation and collateral cascades.
</Callout>
