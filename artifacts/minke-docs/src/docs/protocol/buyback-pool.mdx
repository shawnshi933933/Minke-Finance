# BuybackPool

The BuybackPool is a Phase 1 exit facility that lets VT holders convert their VTs into the underlying token (T) at any time, without waiting for maturity. A protocol fee (e.g. 5%) is charged on each conversion.

## How It Gets Its T

The BuybackPool does not hold a pre-funded reserve of tokens. Instead, it draws T from the vault as tokens vest according to the vesting schedule. As the schedule releases tokens over time, those released tokens become available for BuybackPool conversions.

```
Vault releases tokens per schedule:
  Month 1:  ~4.2% of total vests → available in vault
  Month 2:  ~4.2% of total vests → available in vault
  ...
  Each month's release can be used for BuybackPool conversions
```

This means the BuybackPool's available liquidity grows as the vesting schedule progresses — it is not front-loaded.

## How Conversion Works

During Phase 1, a VT holder can call:

```solidity
function convertViaBuyback(uint256 vtAmount) external;
```

This function:
1. Checks the caller's VT balance
2. Calculates the payout after the protocol fee:

```
payout = vtAmount × (1 − fee)   // e.g. 95 T for every 100 VT converted
fee    = vtAmount × fee_rate     // e.g. 5 T → protocol treasury
```

3. Burns the VT
4. Transfers `payout` T to the caller
5. Routes the fee to the protocol treasury

<Callout type="info" title="No fee on staking or Phase 2">
The protocol fee only applies to BuybackPool conversions. Staking and claiming via `claimT()` carries no fee. Phase 2 redemption via `redeemVT()` is always 1:1 with no fee.
</Callout>

## Price Floor Mechanics

Because the BuybackPool always offers a known conversion rate (1 − fee), it creates an implicit floor price for VTs. Any rational VT holder will use the BuybackPool rather than selling on an AMM if the AMM price falls below `(1 − fee)`:

```
BuybackPool floor ≈ (1 − protocol_fee_rate)
e.g. 0.95 underlying per VT
```

In practice, VT market prices tend to stay above this floor as maturity approaches.

## BuybackPool and AMM Interaction

The two exit mechanisms complement each other:

- **AMMs** offer full liquidity at market-determined prices (may be above or below BuybackPool floor)
- **BuybackPool** offers a guaranteed minimum payout backed by vested tokens from the vault

As the vesting schedule approaches its end, more tokens are available in the vault, increasing BuybackPool capacity. In Phase 2, `redeemVT()` replaces BuybackPool as the redemption mechanism.

## BuybackPool Depletion

If tokens have not yet vested (i.e. the schedule has not released enough T), BuybackPool capacity may be limited early in Phase 1. VT holders can still:

- Sell VTs on AMMs
- Stake and claim Phase 1 pro-rata unlocks via `claimT()`
- Wait for Phase 2 and call `redeemVT()`

<Callout type="info">
The BuybackPool is designed as a supplementary early exit. The primary exits are AMM trading (Phase 1) and 1:1 Phase 2 redemption.
</Callout>
