# DePIN and AI Nodes

Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN) and AI compute node operators earn deterministic token emissions over fixed periods. These are among the fastest-growing DFI categories and a natural fit for Minke.

## The DePIN Emission Model

DePIN protocols bootstrap real-world infrastructure (wireless networks, storage, compute, maps, sensors) by rewarding node operators with tokens. These rewards are:

- **Deterministic** — published emission curves tied to node count, uptime, and tier
- **Long-horizon** — emission periods of 3–10 years are common
- **Illiquid today** — no secondary market for future node rewards

This makes DePIN emissions structurally identical to token vesting: a stream of future tokens with a known schedule and amount.

Each of these follows a published schedule. Minke can tokenize the expected future stream.

## Example: AI Inference Node

An operator running a Bittensor subnet validator expects 10,000 TAO over 36 months:

```
Month 0: Deposit 10,000 TAO future stream
         VTs minted: 9,500 VT (e.g. 95%)
         Operator lists on Uniswap at 0.75 TAO discount

         → Receives 7,125 TAO equivalent today
         
Months 1–36: TAO vests per emission schedule
             VT buyers stake, earn pro-rata TAO unlocks

Month 36: endVesting → Phase 2 → 1:1 redemption
```

The operator funded hardware upgrades or diversified their portfolio at month 0 for a 25% time-discount. The buyer got discounted TAO exposure with defined maturity.

## Why DePIN Is Ideal for Minke

**High operational costs.** DePIN operators have significant upfront hardware costs — GPUs, routers, cameras, storage drives. Accessing present value of future rewards helps finance operations without selling the entire position.

**Long-horizon emissions.** 3–10 year emission schedules are common. Operators don't want to wait years to recover their investment.

**Fragmented, long-tail nodes.** Unlike large protocol treasuries or institutional SAFTs, DePIN nodes are held by individual operators in small quantities. Minke's permissionless approach (no minimums) serves this long tail.

**Growing TVL.** DePIN TVL has grown from near-zero in 2022 to $50B+ in 2025. As the sector matures, liquid secondary markets for node rewards become increasingly important.
