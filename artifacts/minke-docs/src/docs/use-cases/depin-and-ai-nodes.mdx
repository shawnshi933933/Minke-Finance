# DePIN and AI Nodes

Decentralized Physical Infrastructure Networks (DePIN) and AI compute node operators earn deterministic token emissions over fixed periods. These are among the fastest-growing DFI categories and a natural fit for Minke.

## The DePIN Emission Model

DePIN protocols bootstrap real-world infrastructure (wireless networks, storage, compute, maps, sensors) by rewarding node operators with tokens. These rewards are:

- **Deterministic** — published emission curves tied to node count, uptime, and tier
- **Long-horizon** — emission periods of 3–10 years are common
- **Illiquid today** — no secondary market for future node rewards

This creates a constant tension for operators: significant upfront hardware costs (GPUs, antennas, cameras, storage drives) against rewards that trickle in over years. Minke resolves this by letting operators access the present value of their future emission stream.

## Why DePIN Is Ideal for Minke

**High operational costs.** DePIN operators have significant upfront hardware costs — GPUs, routers, cameras, storage drives. Accessing present value of future rewards helps finance operations without waiting years.

**Long-horizon emissions.** 3–10 year emission schedules are common. Operators can't afford to wait that long to recover their hardware investment.

**Fragmented, long-tail nodes.** Unlike large protocol treasuries or institutional SAFTs, DePIN nodes are held by individual operators in small quantities. Minke's permissionless approach (no minimums) serves this long tail.

**Growing TVL.** DePIN TVL has grown from near-zero in 2022 to $50B+ in 2025. As the sector matures, liquid secondary markets for node rewards become increasingly important.
