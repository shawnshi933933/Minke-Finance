# RWA and Bonds

Tokenized real-world assets — government bonds, corporate bonds, trade finance instruments — are the most natural DFI category. They have fixed face value, fixed coupon schedules, and defined maturity dates. Minke provides the secondary market infrastructure these assets are missing.

## Why RWA Bonds Need Minke

The first wave of RWA tokenization (2022–2025) focused on putting bonds **on-chain**. BlackRock's BUIDL, Franklin Templeton's BENJI, Ondo Finance's USDY, and Backed Finance's tokenized Treasuries all represent fixed-income instruments on the blockchain.

But they share a critical limitation: **there is no on-chain secondary market for the underlying cash flows**.

- BUIDL holders can redeem for USDC but cannot sell individual bond cash flows
- T-bill wrappers trade as a single unit; individual coupon streams are invisible
- Institutional buyers who want to hedge duration risk have no on-chain tool

Minke fills this gap.

## How Bond Coupon Tokenisation Works

A tokenized bond wrapper protocol (or its issuer) can integrate Minke to tokenise individual coupon streams:

```
Bond: $10M face, 5% annual coupon, 3-year maturity
Coupon stream: $500K/year × 3 = $1.5M total

Deposit: $1.5M USDC (coupon schedule)
VTs minted: 1,425,000 VT (e.g. 95%)
VTs trade at discount to par reflecting time value
Buyers earn pro-rata coupon distributions
Maturity: Phase 2 redemption for remaining USDC 1:1
```

<Callout type="tip" title="TradFi familiar structure">
Fixed-income investors will recognise this as equivalent to a zero-coupon bond structure. VTs are essentially on-chain zero-coupon notes on the coupon stream.
</Callout>

## Compliance and KYC Considerations

RWA protocols often require KYC/AML compliance for their underlying token holders. Minke is KYC-agnostic at the protocol layer — the VestingToken contract does not enforce any access controls.

Compliance-sensitive deployments can use **transfer restriction hooks** on the underlying token, which Minke's contracts will respect automatically (since VT redemption requires holding the underlying).

For protocols requiring fully permissioned VTs, a custom `VestingToken` variant with ERC-20 transfer restrictions can be deployed. This is on the Minke roadmap.

<Callout type="info">
Minke's initial deployment is permissionless and non-custodial. Regulated use cases should work with the Minke team to understand the appropriate deployment configuration.
</Callout>

## Convergence with TradFi

The long-term thesis is simple: as real-world assets move on-chain, they will need the same financial infrastructure that TradFi fixed-income markets have built over decades:

- **Repo markets** — borrow against bond holdings short-term
- **Futures** — lock in future bond prices today
- **Strip markets** — separate principal and coupons for specialised investors

Minke provides the strip market primitive — and it is the first protocol to do so in a permissionless, oracle-free way.
