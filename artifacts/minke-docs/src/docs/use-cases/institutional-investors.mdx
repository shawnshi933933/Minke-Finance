# Institutional Investors

Minke provides institutional capital with a new tool for managing vesting book risk: on-chain, permissionless, and without counterparty exposure.

## The Institutional Vesting Problem

Institutional crypto investors — VCs, family offices, hedge funds — typically hold large token positions with vesting schedules. These positions pose unique challenges:

- **Concentration risk** — one position can be 20–40% of a fund's NAV
- **Illiquidity at lock-up** — vesting positions cannot be marked to market or exited cleanly
- **Reporting complexity** — LPs need NAV statements that include illiquid positions
- **Regulatory uncertainty** — classified as "unvested" assets in some jurisdictions

OTC vesting desks offer a partial solution but at steep cost (15–40% spread) and with significant counterparty risk.

## How Minke Serves Institutions

### 1. On-Chain Vesting Position Management

Institutions can tokenise their entire vesting book into VTs, creating a standardised, on-chain representation of all future token claims. Benefits:

- Real-time mark-to-market (VTs have observable market prices)
- Transferable and composable (pledge as collateral, structure derivatives)
- Full auditability by LPs and regulators

### 2. Partial Liquidity Without Full Exit

Unlike OTC deals that require selling the entire position, Minke allows partial exits:

```
Institution holds: 10,000,000 VT
Sells 2,000,000 VT on AMM at 0.85 discount
→ Retains 8,000,000 VT (still growing toward par)
→ Received 1,700,000 USDC equivalent today
```

The institution can dial liquidity needs precisely, reducing at the margin rather than exiting entirely.

### 3. Portfolio Construction

VTs open new portfolio strategies:

| Strategy | Mechanism |
|---|---|
| **Duration matching** | Match VT maturity to fund redemption schedule |
| **Basis trading** | Long VT / short underlying spot |
| **Yield pickup** | Stake VTs for pro-rata unlocks vs holding idle |
| **Discount capture** | Buy discounted VTs from distressed sellers |

### 4. LP Reporting and Auditability

Because VTs are on-chain ERC-20 tokens, any LP can verify the fund's vesting exposure by reading the blockchain. This is a significant improvement over the opacity of current OTC vesting desk arrangements.

## Risk Considerations

<Callout type="warning" title="Smart contract risk">
Minke is non-custodial and immutable. Institutions should conduct independent smart contract due diligence and obtain legal opinions on the classification of VTs in their jurisdiction before deploying significant capital.
</Callout>

| Risk | Mitigation |
|---|---|
| Smart contract bugs | Multiple audits; bug bounty program |
| Underlying token price risk | VT price follows underlying; hedge with options |
| Protocol insolvency | Non-custodial; no admin keys |
| Regulatory | Consult counsel; Minke has no KYC requirement |
