# Staking and Mining

Proof-of-Stake validators and proof-of-work miners accumulate future rewards that are structurally similar to vesting schedules: deterministic rates, known schedules, rule-based emissions. Minke tokenises these streams so operators can access their value today.

## Staking Rewards as DFI

A validator running on Ethereum earns a predictable APY (currently ~4%). While individual block rewards have slight variance, the total expected reward over a fixed period is calculable and can be treated as DFI.

The same logic applies to:

- **Ethereum validators** — 32 ETH staked, ~4% APY in ETH
- **Solana delegators** — staking rewards paid every epoch
- **Cosmos validators** — ATOM, OSMO, and IBC chain rewards
- **Binance Smart Chain** — BNB staking emissions

<Callout type="info">
Minke's VT model works best for staking positions where the reward amount and schedule are fixed at commitment time. Liquid staking derivatives (stETH, rETH) represent already-liquid staking positions and require the YT mechanism instead.
</Callout>

## Mining Future Rewards

Bitcoin miners have known revenue curves based on hashrate share, difficulty adjustments, and block rewards. While individual block rewards are probabilistic, a mining operation with stable hashrate can project future BTC income with reasonable confidence.

Some mining entities write binding agreements to receive a fixed BTC amount over a defined period (hash rate agreements). These are pure DFI and ideal Minke candidates.

Similarly, **DePIN mining operations** emit tokens to node operators on fixed schedules. See [DePIN and AI Nodes](/use-cases/depin-and-ai-nodes) for details.

## Example: ETH Validator Pool

A validator pool operator has 1,000 ETH staked and expects 40 ETH in rewards over the next 12 months. They need capital now — hardware upgrades, operational costs — but don't want to sell their staking position.

```
── OPERATOR (originator) ──────────────────────────────────────
Setup:  Creates Tranche (12-month linear), deposits 40 ETH into Vault
        Stakes allocation NFT → Vault mints 40 ETH-VT (1:1)

Goal:   Get liquidity today
Action: Sells 40 ETH-VT on AMM at 0.93 ETH per VT
Result: Receives ~37.2 ETH today ✓
        (Cost of liquidity: 0.07 ETH per VT — 7% discount on par)

── BUYER (investor) ───────────────────────────────────────────
Buys:   40 ETH-VT at 0.93 ETH per VT → pays ~37.2 ETH

Option A: Stake in BuybackPool
          → Receives ETH gradually over 12 months as vault releases
          → e.g. ~3.17 ETH/month × 0.95 = ~3.01 ETH/month (after e.g. 5% fee)
          → Total ~36.1 ETH over 12 months (vs 37.2 ETH paid) — lower return
             due to fee, but faster than waiting for Phase 2

Option B: Hold to Phase 2
          → vestingEnded = true at Month 12
          → redeemVT(40 VT) → 40 ETH (1:1, no fee)
          → Earns the full 2.8 ETH discount as profit
```

## Key Advantages for Stakers/Miners

| Benefit | Detail |
|---|---|
| **No unstaking required** | The validator position remains fully intact |
| **Operational capital** | Fund hardware purchases against future rewards |
| **Portfolio diversification** | Convert ETH yield exposure into stablecoins or other assets |
| **No lock-up broken** | The underlying rewards still arrive per schedule in the Vault |

<Callout type="warning" title="Variable staking rewards">
Staking rewards that depend on utilisation rates, validator count changes, or protocol governance decisions are VFI — they are better suited for Minke Phase 2 (Yield Token). Only use Phase 1 VT for staking positions with deterministic, pre-committed schedules.
</Callout>
