# Protocol Treasuries

On-chain protocol treasuries face a structural challenge: they hold billions in native tokens with future vesting schedules but cannot access present value without creating market selling pressure. Minke solves this.

## The Treasury Dilemma

Most DeFi protocols allocate 20–40% of token supply to a treasury, typically vesting over 2–5 years. This treasury is supposed to fund operations, grants, and growth — but it sits illiquid while the protocol needs cash for:

- Engineering salaries (paid in stablecoins)
- Security audits
- Marketing and ecosystem grants
- Regulatory and legal costs

The naive solution — sell native tokens on the open market — creates immediate selling pressure and signals weakness. DAOs have learned this lesson repeatedly.

## How Minke Helps

The treasury mints VTs against its vesting allocation and **sells them** to secondary buyers. The treasury gets stablecoin liquidity today. VT buyers get discounted access to the protocol's future token stream.

This is not a loan. There is no repayment obligation. The treasury has simply monetised a portion of its future vesting schedule.

<Callout type="info" title="DAO governance compatible">
Minke's interaction requires a standard multisig transaction. Any DAO using Gnosis Safe, Llama, or similar tooling can interact with Minke contracts without custom integrations.
</Callout>


## Vesting VTs to Contributors

Minke-native vesting is a powerful alternative to traditional token grants:

Instead of granting contributors raw tokens with a cliff + linear lock, protocols can:

1. Create a Minke tranche with the contributor's allocation
2. Grant the contributor VTs directly at day one
3. The contributor holds liquid, tradable tokens from grant day — no waiting for cliff

The underlying tokens still vest per the original schedule. Contributors who want early liquidity can sell VTs at a discount; those who believe in the protocol hold to Phase 2 and redeem at par.

## Treasury Risk Management

| Traditional Treasury | Minke-Enhanced Treasury |
|---|---|
| All future tokens illiquid | Portion tokenized as VTs and sold for stablecoins |
| Must sell spot tokens for operations | VTs sold OTC/AMM with no direct market impact |
| Opaque future valuation | VTs have observable on-chain market prices |
| Grants are locked — no contributor liquidity | Contributor VTs are liquid from day one |

## Protocol Integration

The Minke team works directly with DAO treasuries to structure appropriate tranches. Considerations include:

- **Tranche size** — typically 10–20% of total unvested treasury per tranche
- **Schedule** — match Minke tranche to the treasury's actual vesting schedule
- **Governance approval** — propose via DAO forum and execute via multisig
