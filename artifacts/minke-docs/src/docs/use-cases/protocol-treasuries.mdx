# Protocol Treasuries

On-chain protocol treasuries face a structural challenge: they hold billions in native tokens with future vesting schedules but cannot access present value without creating market selling pressure. Minke solves this.

## The Treasury Dilemma

Most DeFi protocols allocate 20–40% of token supply to a treasury, typically vesting over 2–5 years. This treasury is supposed to fund operations, grants, and growth — but it sits illiquid while the protocol needs cash for:

- Engineering salaries (paid in stablecoins)
- Security audits
- Marketing and ecosystem grants
- Regulatory and legal costs

The naive solution — sell native tokens — creates immediate selling pressure and signals weakness. DAOs have learned this lesson repeatedly.

## Minke for Treasury Management

### Convert Vesting Treasury Into Liquid Instrument

A DAO governance proposal can authorise the treasury multisig to:

1. Deposit a portion of the unvested treasury allocation into Minke
2. Receive VTs representing the future token stream
3. Sell VTs on a secondary market at a time-discount

This achieves liquidity for operational needs without market-selling the native token. The underlying token remains vesting on schedule — it's just the *claim* that has been tokenized.

<Callout type="info" title="DAO governance compatible">
Minke's interaction requires a standard multisig transaction. Any DAO using Gnosis Safe, Llama, or similar tooling can interact with Minke contracts without custom integrations.
</Callout>

### Example: Ecosystem Fund

A DeFi protocol has 50M TOKEN in its ecosystem fund, vesting linearly over 4 years. It needs $2M USDC for the next 12 months of operations.

```
Treasury holds: 50,000,000 TOKEN vesting over 48 months

Step 1: Deposit 12.5M TOKEN (12-month tranche)
        VTs minted: 11,875,000 VT (95%)

Step 2: Sell 11,875,000 VT on Uniswap at 0.92
        → Receives ~10.9M USDC equivalent

Step 3: Use USDC for operations, grants, audits

Remaining 48-month treasury: unaffected
VT buyers earn monthly TOKEN unlocks from the sold tranche
```

The protocol funded 12 months of operations at a 7–8% cost of capital — far cheaper than equity dilution or stablecoin debt.

### Vesting VTs to Contributors

Minke-native vesting is a powerful alternative to traditional vesting:

Instead of granting contributors raw tokens with a cliff + linear schedule, protocols can:

1. Create a Minke tranche with the contributor's allocation
2. Grant the contributor VTs at day one
3. The contributor receives VTs immediately — liquid, tradable, compostable

This provides contributors real economic value at grant time while the underlying continues to vest.

## Treasury Risk Management

| Traditional Treasury | Minke-Enhanced Treasury |
|---|---|
| All future tokens illiquid | Portion tokenized as VTs |
| Sell pressure if operations need funds | VTs sold OTC without direct market impact |
| No yield on unvested allocation | Stake VTs for pro-rata unlocks |
| Opaque valuation | VTs have observable market prices |

## Protocol Integration

The Minke team works directly with DAO treasuries to structure appropriate tranches. Considerations include:

- **Tranche size** — typically 10–20% of total unvested treasury per tranche
- **Schedule** — match Minke tranche to the treasury's actual vesting schedule
- **Governance approval** — propose via DAO forum and execute via multisig
