# SAFTs and Team Vesting

Simple Agreements for Future Tokens (SAFTs) and team/advisor vesting allocations are the highest-concentration DFI category in crypto — and the most natural fit for Minke.

## The Status Quo

Most token launches allocate 15–40% of total supply to team, advisors, and investors, vesting over 2–4 years with a 12-month cliff. This creates a structural problem:

- **Teams** are locked in for years with significant personal wealth concentrated in one illiquid asset
- **Investors** who want early returns must negotiate OTC deals at 20–40% discounts
- **Protocols** face ongoing selling pressure as vesting unlocks create market overhangs

None of this is necessary. Minke provides an alternative that preserves vesting terms while unlocking present value.

## How Minke Solves It

A team member with a 4-year vesting schedule can:

1. Stake their allocation NFT in the Vault to receive VTs 1:1
2. List VTs on a secondary market at a time-discount for immediate liquidity
3. Or stake VTs in the BuybackPool to receive T gradually as it vests (e.g. 5% protocol fee)
4. Or hold to maturity and redeem at 1:1 in Phase 2 — no fee

<Callout type="tip" title="Vesting terms preserved">
The underlying tokens remain in the Minke contract on the original vesting schedule. From the protocol's perspective, nothing changes. Only the economic claim has been tokenized and made liquid.
</Callout>

## Example Flow

Suppose an advisor holds 500,000 TOKEN vesting linearly over 24 months:

```
Month 0:  Project creates Tranche (24-month linear), deposits 500,000 TOKEN into Vault
          Advisor stakes allocation NFT → Vault mints 500,000 VT (1:1)

Option A: Sell on AMM
          Advisor sells 500,000 VT at 0.82 market discount
          → Receives 410,000 USDC immediately

Option B: Stake in BuybackPool (e.g. 5% fee on distributions)
          Advisor stakes 500,000 VT in BuybackPool
          → Receives T gradually each month as vault releases per schedule
          → e.g. receives ~19,792 T/month × 0.95 = ~18,802 T/month (after fee)

Month 1–24: TOKEN vests on schedule → BP pulls from Vault → burns VT → distributes T
            
Month 24: vestingEnded = true
          Remaining VT holders call redeemVT() → 1:1 TOKEN (no fee)
```

## SAFT-Specific Considerations

SAFTs are typically larger ($1M–$100M) and have more complex vesting terms (cliff + linear, multiple tranches). Minke handles these via:

| SAFT Feature | Minke Mechanism |
|---|---|
| Cliff period | VTs are issued but Phase 1 distributions begin only after cliff |
| Linear unlock | Checkpoints distribute pro-rata unlocks each period |
| Multiple tranches | Multiple `VestingToken` contracts, one per tranche |
| Token Generation Event | Deposit occurs at TGE; schedule starts from that block |

## Benefits for Protocols

Protocols that adopt Minke for their vesting infrastructure gain:

- **Reduced selling pressure** — holders get liquidity without market selling
- **Alignment signaling** — the underlying still vests; recipients haven't exited their belief in the protocol
- **Institutional-grade tooling** — large funds and family offices can manage SAFT exposure through a standardised on-chain instrument
