# What is Minke?

Minke Finance is a DeFi protocol that makes the time value of money tradable on-chain. By wrapping any vested or staked position into a fungible **Vesting Token (VT)**, Minke lets holders access the present value of their future claims — without waiting for lock-up periods to expire.

<Callout type="tip" title="Core mission">
Making future income positions as liquid and composable as any other on-chain asset.
</Callout>

## The Central Problem Minke Solves

Most DeFi wealth sits in the future. Team tokens vest over four years. Staking rewards accumulate over months. RWA bonds mature in 12–36 months. Node licenses emit tokens over years.

Holders of these positions face a hard trade-off:

- **Wait** — and accept full illiquidity
- **Sell the underlying** — and forfeit the entire position

Minke introduces a third option: **tokenize the future claim, trade the token today**.

## How the Protocol Works

When you deposit a future-income position into Minke Protocol, you receive a **Vesting Token (VT)** — an ERC-20 representing a 1:1 claim on the underlying asset.

VTs are:
- **Fungible** — any VT from the same tranche is identical
- **Tradable** — list on any AMM or OTC desk immediately
- **Composable** — use as collateral, borrow against them, or earn yield
- **Redeemable** — present the VT at maturity for the underlying 1:1 (plus excess)

```
Holder → deposits future position → smart contract → mints VT
Buyer  → acquires VT at discount → trades, holds or stakes → claims at maturity
```

## Liquidity Solution

Traditional DeFi offers two extremes. Minke creates a new middle path:

| Approach | Liquidity | Full Upside |
|---|---|---|
| Hold and wait | None | Yes |
| Sell underlying | Immediate | No |
| **Minke VT** | **Immediate** | **Yes (via VT price)** |

The protocol is non-custodial, oracle-free, and permissionless — any team, protocol, or institution can create a tranche for liquidity.

## Who It's For

- **Token holders** with vested allocations who need present liquidity
- **Protocols** that want to offer more attractive vesting structures to contributors
- **Buyers** seeking discounted exposure to high-conviction assets with a defined maturity
- **Institutional desks** managing large vesting book risk

<Callout type="info">
Minke is chain-agnostic and EVM-compatible. Initial deployment targets Ethereum mainnet and EVM L2s.
</Callout>
