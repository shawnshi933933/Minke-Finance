# Key Concepts

This page defines the core terminology used throughout Minke's documentation.

## Asset Types

### DFI — Deterministic Future Income

A future income stream where the amount and schedule are fixed and known in advance. Examples:
- Token vesting schedules (cliff + linear)
- SAFT / investor allocation tranches
- Node license emission schedules
- Mining reward agreements

DFI is the primary asset class Minke Phase 1 handles. Because the schedule is deterministic, no oracle is needed.

### VFI — Variable Future Income

A future income stream where the amount depends on external factors — utilisation rates, price feeds, governance decisions. Examples:
- Liquid staking yields (stETH)
- AMM LP fees
- Lending protocol interest

VFI requires Minke Phase 2 (Yield Token). Phase 1 VT is not suitable for VFI.

## Protocol Primitives

### Vesting Suite

A project-level deployment consisting of a `Vault` and `ERC1155NFT` contract, created via `VestingFactory`. Each project has one suite; within it, the project owner creates individual tranches.

### Tranche

A specific vesting allocation within a suite, identified by a `trancheId`. Each tranche has:
- A fixed vesting schedule (start, cliff, end, total amount)
- Its own `VTToken` (ERC-20)
- Its own `BuybackPool`

### ERC-1155 Allocation NFT

A token representing a recipient's vesting allocation. The project owner mints these and distributes them to recipients. Staking an ERC-1155 NFT in the Vault mints VT to the holder at a fixed rate (`vtPerNft`).

### VT — Vesting Token

A fungible ERC-20 minted when recipients stake their ERC-1155 allocation NFTs in the Vault. VTs represent a claim on the underlying token at vesting maturity. They are:
- Minted only by the Vault (on NFT stake)
- Burned only by the BuybackPool (on T pulls, or Phase 2 redemption)
- Freely tradable as standard ERC-20

### YT — Yield Token (Phase 2)

A future token type designed for VFI streams where yield is variable. Not yet live — see the roadmap for planned release.

### BuybackPool

The staking and distribution contract deployed alongside each VTToken. VT holders stake VT here; the pool automatically pulls T from the Vault per the release schedule, burns VT 1:1 as T arrives, and distributes T to stakers proportionally. A protocol fee (e.g. 5%) is deducted from each distribution. After vesting ends, `redeemVT()` enables 1:1 Phase 2 redemption from the pool's `excessT` reserve.

## Staking Mechanics

### Shares

When a user stakes VT in the BuybackPool, they receive shares at the current share-to-VT ratio. As VT is burned during T pulls, share value (in VT terms) decreases. Withdrawals from the BuybackPool are denominated in shares, not VT amount.

### accVTPerShare

A global accumulator tracking cumulative VT burned per share. Increases each time a T pull occurs and VT is burned. Used to calculate each staker's proportional pending claimable T.

### claimableT

The T amount accrued to a staker, calculated as their proportional share of all T pulled since their last interaction, minus the protocol fee. Transferred to the staker when they call `claimT()`.

## Economic Terms

### Present Value (PV)

The market-determined value today of a future cash flow. In Minke, VTs trade at a discount to par (1 underlying) until maturity. The discount reflects time value and risk.

### excessT

T accumulated in the BuybackPool when more T arrives from the Vault than VT can be burned (e.g. near dust protection limits). Reserved for Phase 2 `redeemVT()` at 1:1.

### Protocol Fee

A fee (e.g. 5%) deducted from T distributions to BuybackPool stakers. Set as `userRewardRate` in basis points (e.g. 950 = 95% to stakers, 5% to protocol). Immutable after tranche deployment. Not charged at NFT staking, VT minting, or Phase 2 redemption.

<Callout type="note">
The fee rate is fixed at contract deployment. There is no admin key that can change fee parameters post-deploy.
</Callout>
