# Market Opportunity

Minke is building the primitive layer for a $1T+ asset class that has no on-chain present-value market today.

## The Unaddressed Market

Every major financial market has a mechanism for trading future cash flows at present value:

- **TradFi bonds**: yield curve + bond futures + repo market
- **Equity options**: Black-Scholes pricing + CBOE listed options
- **Credit markets**: CDS, CLOs, securitisation vehicles

**DeFi has none of this for vesting and future-income positions.**

The closest analog — OTC vesting desks — operates off-chain with 15–40% spreads and zero transparency. Minke replaces this with permissionless, on-chain price discovery.

This segment alone represents an estimated **$50–100B notional** that could be wrapped in Minke tranches.

## Why Minke Wins

**Oracle-free architecture.** Because DFI schedules are deterministic, Minke doesn't need a price feed. This removes the #1 systemic risk in DeFi — oracle manipulation.

**1:1 backing at maturity.** VT holders have a mathematical guarantee of par redemption at maturity (less the 5% protocol fee). This is a first in DeFi future-income primitives.

**Permissionless composability.** Any protocol can create a tranche with no permission required. VTs immediately slot into existing AMM, lending, and structured-product infrastructure.

## Timing

Three macro tailwinds accelerate Minke's go-to-market:

### 1. RWA Acceleration
BlackRock's BUIDL, Franklin Templeton's BENJI, and dozens of tokenized treasury protocols have moved >$10B of real-world cash flows on-chain. These are all DFI. None have a liquid secondary market.

### 2. Restaking Maturation
EigenLayer has crossed $15B TVL. Operators earn complex, multi-source yield streams. Minke can tokenise and strip these streams for the first time.

### 3. Institutional DeFi
Compliance-conscious capital wants fixed-maturity instruments. Minke's VT format — fixed schedule, deterministic payout — maps directly to institutional fixed-income needs.
