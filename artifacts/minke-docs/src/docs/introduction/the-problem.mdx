# The Problem

There is over **$1 trillion of on-chain future income** with no present-value infrastructure. Vested token allocations, staking rewards, RWA bonds, and DePIN node emissions are all promises of future cash flows — but the DeFi stack treats them as if they don't exist until maturity.

## Three Structural Pain Points

### 1. Illiquidity

Future-income positions cannot be monetised before maturity without surrendering the entire position. A team member holding a 4-year vesting schedule must choose between waiting and forfeiting. There is no middle path.

The scale of this problem is significant:

- Exchange employees, investors, and advisors hold hundreds of billions in unvested tokens
- PoS validators receive staking rewards over multi-year horizons
- RWA bond issuers lock coupon streams in smart contracts with no secondary market

### 2. Non-Tradable Positions

Even when a vesting position has clear economic value, it cannot be priced or transferred on-chain. There is no standardised representation for "future claim on 10,000 USDC in 18 months". Without a fungible format, there is no market.

The result: off-chain OTC desks charge 15–40% spreads on vesting discounts. This is a value leak that accrues to intermediaries, not holders.

<Callout type="warning" title="The spread problem">
OTC desks facilitating early vesting exits extract 15–40% of notional value in spread. Minke replaces this with on-chain price discovery.
</Callout>

### 3. Broken Price Discovery

Because vested positions cannot be traded on-chain, token markets ignore them entirely. This creates a systematic distortion:

**Fully Diluted Valuation (FDV) vs Circulating Supply** is one of crypto's most debated metrics. The gap exists because vested and unvested tokens are structurally different instruments — one is a present asset, one is a future claim.

Minke creates the infrastructure to make this distinction visible and tradeable on-chain, bringing DeFi closer to how fixed-income and equity derivative markets work in TradFi.
