## FAQ Preview

**What wallets are supported?**
Any EVM-compatible wallet. MetaMask, Rainbow, Coinbase Wallet, and Safe multisigs are all compatible.

**Is there a minimum deposit?**
No minimum at the protocol level. The Minke app may suggest a minimum for gas efficiency.

**What happens to my tokens after I deposit?**
They are held in a non-custodial smart contract. Neither Minke nor any third party can withdraw them. Only the vesting schedule governs their release.

**Can I get my tokens back before maturity?**
You can sell your VTs on an AMM, use the BuybackPool, or wait for Phase 2. Direct early withdrawal is not possible — this is by design.
