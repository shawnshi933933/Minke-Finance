## Frequently Asked Questions

A selection of common questions. A full FAQ will be published at mainnet launch.

### General

**What is Minke?**
Minke Finance is a protocol that converts illiquid vesting positions and future-income streams into tradable ERC-20 tokens (VTs). See [What is Minke?](/introduction/what-is-minke) for a full explanation.

**Is Minke audited?**
Audit reports will be published before mainnet launch. See [Deployments](/technical-reference/deployments) for current status.

**Who controls the protocol?**
Minke's deployed contracts are immutable — no admin can modify terms post-deploy. The `ContractRegistry` owner (a timelocked multisig) can authorize new factory contracts.

**Is there a Minke token?**
There is no MINKE governance token at launch. Future governance plans are described in [Long-Term Vision](/vision/long-term).

### Protocol Mechanics

**What is a VT?**
A Vesting Token — a fungible ERC-20 representing a 1:1 claim on the underlying asset at vesting maturity. See [Vesting Token (VT)](/protocol/vesting-token).

**Can I lose money using Minke?**
If you sell VTs at a discount before maturity, you give up some value. If you hold VTs to maturity, you receive the underlying 1:1. The main risk is smart contract risk (bugs) and underlying token price risk.

**What is the protocol fee for?**
It funds the BuybackPool (exit liquidity in Phase 1) and protocol operations. It is collected once at deposit and never again. See [Fee Structure](/protocol/fee-structure).

**Why does Minke not need an oracle?**
DFI vesting schedules are deterministic — the contract knows exactly how many tokens vest at each moment without an external price feed. This is by design. See [Design Principles](/protocol/design-principles).

### Trading VTs

**Where can I buy or sell VTs?**
VTs are standard ERC-20 tokens tradable on any DEX. Minke will maintain official liquidity pools on supported AMMs.

**Are VTs transferable?**
Yes, fully. There are no transfer restrictions on VTs.

**What determines the VT price?**
Market supply and demand, anchored by the 1:1 redemption guarantee at maturity. VTs trade at a discount to par that reflects time value and risk.
